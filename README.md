### 🚀 Featured Projects

- [**GNN Geometry Detector**](https://github.com/vkrm1612/GNN-Geometry-Detector): A graph neural network (GraphSAGE) that classifies mechanical CAD parts (bolts, nuts, flanges, pressure vessels) directly from STEP B-Rep topology. It matches a LoRA-fine-tuned Llama-3.1-8B with a tiny fraction of the parameters.
- [**Porous Aerofoil Study**](https://github.com/vkrm1612/Porus_Aerofoil): Porous vs. solid NACA 0012 in ANSYS Fluent across Re = 2,000–200,000, with up to **+168% lift** and **−93% drag**. Forces are recovered with an in-house control-volume momentum-balance tool.
- [**Fuselage Structural Analysis**](https://github.com/vkrm1612/Preliminary-Sizing/tree/main/Fuselage): Semi-monocoque sizing and Abaqus validation of a 38.4 m commercial fuselage, with 32+ FEA material combinations across 8 aerospace alloys.
- [**Lattice Boltzmann Solver**](https://github.com/vkrm1612/Portfolio/tree/main/CFD/LBM_Solver): A D2Q9 BGK solver in Python that captures the onset of von Kármán vortex shedding.
- [**2D Linear Convection**](https://github.com/vkrm1612/Portfolio/tree/main/CFD/Linear_Convection): The same PDE solved two ways, with a Fortran upwind finite-difference scheme and a PyTorch physics-informed neural network (PINN).
- [**MPU-6050 I2C Reader**](https://github.com/vkrm1612/Portfolio/tree/main/embedded_systems/MPU_6050): A library-free 6-axis IMU driver for Arduino that reads hardware registers directly over I2C.
- ...and more! See my pinned repos and [portfolio](https://vkrm1612.github.io).
